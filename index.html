Runna → WorkOutDoors

Bring a structured Runna session onto your wrist in WorkOutDoors — reps, recoveries, paces and route.

1 Session

Paste the session from Runna — the workout title and the step lines (Warm-up, Set, Rest, Cool down). Any of the usual formats work.

    Pace assumptions

    Runna gives one pace per rep; WorkOutDoors wants a range, so a single pace becomes target ± this. Easy and walk paces are only used to estimate how far you'll travel during timed steps (e.g. a 90s walk) so route cues land in the right place.

      Route with step cues

      Route + a cue at every rep, recovery and the finish. Records are timed to your planned paces. Needs a route.

      Interval schedule

      The exact steps to set up in WorkOutDoors → Settings → Interval Schedules, so the watch runs the session with pace alerts.

      FIT workout

      Structured workout (steps, repeats, pace ranges) in the standard format. Not currently imported by WorkOutDoors, but works with Garmin, COROS and others.

      How to get it onto your watch
      1. Route: on your iPhone, tap FIT course (or Share…), then open the file in WorkOutDoors — from the share sheet, or via Files → the file → Share → WorkOutDoors. In WorkOutDoors, sync the route to the watch from the Routes list.
      2. Schedule: open the schedule card, then in WorkOutDoors on iPhone go to Settings → Interval Schedules → + and add the steps as shown. Repeats map to a repeat block; nested repeats are already unrolled for you.
      3. On the watch: pick the route on the map, choose a running workout, select the interval schedule, and start. Cues fire as you reach each step's start point; the schedule drives the step timing and pace alerts.
      4. Splits: set WorkOutDoors' auto-lap to off (or per-interval) for interval sessions so lap splits line up with Runna's reps; for easy/long runs, 1 km / 1 mi auto-laps match Runna's splits.

      Time-based steps (e.g. 90s walk) are placed on the route using your estimated pace, so their cues are approximate (marked ≈). Distance steps are exact.
